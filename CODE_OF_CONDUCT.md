# Code of conduct

open-reel follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people of every
background and level of experience, and keep criticism about the work, not the
person. Harassment, insults, discrimination and sexual content are not
accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project
meets.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, tell the maintainers privately at
{{CONTACT}}. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainers decide what to do,
from a warning to a ban from the project, following the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
